INVESTMENTS

Long-term impact
over short-term trends.

Dominic Thiem invests selectively in companies that connect innovation, sustainability, health, sport, and community. Every investment follows a clear principle: long-term value over short-term trends.

His investments are driven by personal conviction, hands-on experience, and a clear ambition to support projects that create a positive impact on everyday life, health, and the future.

One of Dominic's most recent investments in health, prevention, and longevity. Sport · Technology · Community

Hut & Stiel

Sustainable Nutrition · Urban Lifestyle

Hut & Stiel combines sustainable food production with urban lifestyle and modern gastronomy. The company stands for regionality, conscious consumption, and a renewed appreciation of food. Dominic discovered the project around ten years ago. Mushroom cultivation continues to fascinate him - technologically and ecologically. Mushrooms are a key building block of future sustainable nutrition systems.

NEOH

Better Sweets · Health

NEOH represents a new generation of confectionery - with no added sugar and no compromise on taste. As Dominic's first investment, the brand holds special significance: he has been involved from the very beginning and continues to support its development with strong conviction. For Dominic, it is not about restriction, but about making conscious choices - especially off the court. A balanced approach to nutrition is key for long-term wellbeing and everyday performance. NEOH shows that enjoyment and a mindful approach to sugar can go hand in hand.

An investment in modern nutrition and sustainable food innovation. Dominic supports the company with strong conviction, as conscious nutrition is a key driver of performance, wellbeing, and long-term health. For him, it is a project with substance, clear vision, and lasting relevance.
